# Voople asset policy

`LICENSE` applies to the software source code in this repository. It does not
automatically license every image, animation, sound, screenshot, user upload,
store item, profile customization, or other non-code work under AGPL.

## Default rules

- Voople logos, application icons, marketing artwork, screenshots, and
  first-party customization assets are reserved unless an adjacent license or
  provenance manifest grants additional rights.
- Third-party works keep their original licenses and attribution requirements.
  Voople does not claim ownership of them.
- User and creator uploads remain subject to their authors' rights and the
  applicable product terms. Repository visibility is not permission to reuse
  them.
- An asset with unknown provenance must not be added to an official release or
  offered for sale until its source and permissions are recorded.

## Generated assets

AI-assisted assets must record the tool, date, responsible human editor, source
inputs, and any relevant service terms. They are treated as first-party assets
only to the extent the project owner actually holds enforceable rights. Because
generated output may be non-unique and copyrightability depends on jurisdiction
and human contribution, generation alone is not a guarantee of exclusivity.

## Creator program

Before accepting creator assets, Voople needs separate Creator Terms covering
ownership, the licence granted to Voople, editing and animation rights,
commercial use and revenue share, moderation, takedowns, refunds, and what
happens when a creator leaves. AGPL is not the default licence for those works.

If you want to reuse an asset and cannot find an explicit licence next to it,
assume that no reuse permission has been granted and ask the project owner.
