# Contributing to Voople

Voople is maintainer-led while its architecture, product contracts, and legal
foundation are still being completed.

Issues, reproducible bug reports, design analysis, and security-safe test cases
are welcome. Please read `AGENTS.md`, the canonical product documents, and the
delivery matrix before proposing code.

## Code contributions

The project intends to remain available under AGPL while retaining an option to
offer separately licensed official and partner distributions. That requires a
reviewed contributor agreement which permits dual licensing and a DCO sign-off
which confirms the origin of each contribution.

Until that contributor agreement is published and its acceptance is recorded,
substantial external code or asset pull requests will not be merged. This avoids
asking contributors to accept improvised legal language and protects both their
rights and the project's future licensing model.

## Security

Do not open a public issue for a vulnerability, leaked credential, private media
URL, or personal data. Follow `SECURITY.md` instead.

## Small changes

Documentation corrections and other non-substantial changes may be accepted at
the maintainer's discretion, but must still have clear provenance and must not
include third-party content without permission.
